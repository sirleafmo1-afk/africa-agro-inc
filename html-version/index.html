About Africa Agro Inc

Founded in 2009, Africa Agro Inc provides end-to-end solutions that connect Liberian cocoa farmers with international markets while upholding the highest standards of sustainability, quality, and compliance.

Location: Pacoyard, Freeport, Monrovia, Liberia

Founder & CEO

Mohamed Sirleaf leads Africa Agro Inc with a vision of sustainable cocoa production and farmer empowerment. With years of experience in agricultural supply chains, he is committed to building transparent, ethical partnerships that create lasting impact for Liberian farmers and international buyers.

Vision: To establish Africa Agro Inc as a trusted partner in sustainable cocoa supply, driving economic development in Liberia while preserving its forests and supporting farmer livelihoods.

What We Offer

We provide end-to-end solutions that connect Liberian cocoa farmers with international markets while upholding the highest standards of sustainability, quality, and compliance.

🌱 Sustainable Cocoa Supply

  • Reliable volumes of high-quality Theobroma cacao
  • Eco-friendly and regenerative practices
  • Shade-grown and agroforestry cocoa
  • Supports biodiversity and forest protection
  • Flexible supply options for all market types

✅ Traceability & Compliance

  • Full farm-to-export traceability with geolocation
  • EU Deforestation Regulation (EUDR) documentation
  • International sustainability frameworks
  • Transparent reporting for buyer audits
  • Simplifies regulatory requirements

👨‍🌾 Farmer Training & Capacity Building

  • Good agricultural practices training
  • Climate-smart techniques
  • Agroforestry systems support
  • Soil health improvement
  • Post-harvest handling guidance

🎯 Quality Assurance

  • Professional fermentation and drying
  • International quality standards
  • Rigorous quality control
  • Food-safety protocols
  • Hygiene standards for global buyers

✈️ Export Logistics & Market Access

  • Efficient export coordination
  • International documentation
  • Shipping support
  • Direct buyer connections
  • Sustainability data sharing

🤝 Partnership & Impact Programs

  • Living income focus
  • Community development
  • Environmental restoration
  • Customized sustainability programs
  • Long-term relationship building

Whether you are a chocolate maker, trader, or brand seeking deforestation-free, high-quality Liberian cocoa—or a partner looking to strengthen sustainable sourcing—we deliver reliable supply backed by integrity, transparency, and real impact on the ground.
